# Market Analysis & Competitor Strategy

## Overview
A strategic business prompt designed to evaluate competitive landscapes, benchmark primary industry rivals, and produce structured strategic recommendations.

## Primary Use Cases
- Benchmarking product positioning prior to go-to-market execution.
- Conducting competitive intelligence for executive summaries and pitch decks.
- Identifying underserved market segments and strategic differentiation opportunities.

## Framework & Technique
- **Framework:** R-T-F (Role, Task, Format) with Context and Constraints.
- **Technique:** Zero-Shot Chain-of-Thought (CoT).

## Quick Start
1. Open `prompt.md`.
2. Fill in `[INSERT PRODUCT/SERVICE NAME]`, `[INSERT TARGET AUDIENCE]`, and `[INSERT COMPETITORS]`.
3. Execute the prompt in your target LLM interface.
