## Designed Prompt Execution
**Input Parameters:**
- Subject: Cellular Biology
- Target Academic Level: AP High School
- Notes: Glycolysis happens in cytoplasm (makes 2 ATP, no oxygen needed). Krebs cycle in mitochondrial matrix (makes 2 ATP, needs oxygen). ETC in inner mitochondrial membrane (makes ~32 ATP, needs oxygen).

**Output:**

### 1. Core Concepts Summary
- **Glycolysis:** Anaerobic process occurring in the cytoplasm that breaks down glucose into pyruvate, producing a net yield of 2 ATP.
- **Krebs Cycle (Citric Acid Cycle):** Aerobic process occurring in the mitochondrial matrix that processes pyruvate to generate 2 ATP alongside electron carriers.
- **Electron Transport Chain (ETC):** Aerobic process occurring on the inner mitochondrial membrane that uses electron gradients to produce ~32 ATP.

### 2. Key Terminology Table
| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Anaerobic** | Biological processes that occur in the absence of molecular oxygen. |
| **Aerobic** | Biological processes that require oxygen to proceed. |
| **Mitochondrial Matrix** | The innermost compartment of the mitochondrion where the Krebs cycle occurs. |

### 3. Practice Quiz
1. Which stage of cellular respiration occurs in the cytoplasm without requiring oxygen?
   - A) Krebs Cycle
   - B) Glycolysis
   - C) Electron Transport Chain
   *Answer: B — Glycolysis is an anaerobic process taking place in the cytoplasm.*

2. What is the approximate maximum total yield of ATP produced from one molecule of glucose across all three stages?
   - A) 2 ATP
   - B) 4 ATP
   - C) 36 ATP
   *Answer: C — Glycolysis (2) + Krebs Cycle (2) + ETC (~32) yields approximately 36 ATP.*

3. Explain why the Electron Transport Chain depends on oxygen.
   *Answer: Oxygen acts as the final electron acceptor at the end of the transport chain; without it, electrons accumulate and ATP synthesis stops.*
