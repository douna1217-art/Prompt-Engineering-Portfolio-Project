# About Me
> Aspiring software and AI solutions engineer focused on prompt architecture, model optimization, and building production-ready LLM workflows.

## Who I Am
I am a developer interested in applied artificial intelligence and natural language processing. My work focuses on closing the gap between raw LLM foundation models and high-precision business, educational, and software engineering tools through systematic prompt engineering.

## My Interest in AI
My primary interest lies in prompt architecture, reasoning optimization, and structured output design. I focus on developing repeatable prompting strategies—such as Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, Few-Shot exemplars, and structural constraint enforcement—that reduce hallucinations and deliver reliable technical outputs.

## Skills and Focus Areas
- **Prompt Architecture:** Designing production templates using structural frameworks (R-T-F, C-A-R-E).
- **Advanced Prompting Techniques:** Implementing Chain-of-Thought (CoT), Zero-Shot CoT, and Few-Shot conditioning.
- **LLM Benchmarking & Evaluation:** Conducting comparative testing (Naive vs. Designed) and iterative optimization based on qualitative metrics.
- **Developer Workflows:** Writing clean technical documentation, maintaining version-controlled Git repositories, and managing public API integrations.

## Goals
I aim to pursue further studies in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, targeting roles in AI engineering and backend systems design. My objective is to build accessible, reliable automated tools that streamline complex technical and decision-making processes.

## What You Will Find in This Portfolio
This portfolio contains production-ready prompt frameworks across three domain categories:
- **Business:** `prompts/business/market-analysis/` (Competitive strategy & market evaluation)

- `methodology.md`: Design rationales, part-by-part justifications, and evaluator benchmarks.
- `examples/sample_outputs.md`: Side-by-side comparative evidence showing Naive vs. Designed prompt performance.
